WHO I REALLY AM

My name is Matthew Bolanos-Chavez, and I am the one behind Megalo_64. As far back as I could remember, I have always had so many ideas for games, shows, and movies. There have been many forms of content responsible for fueling my creativity since childhood. From my childhood video games, to YouTube videos of the past and present, it was everywhere. Nowadays, I am far more aware of the projects I want to create the most and how I would create them. This is who I am in a nutshell.

MY REASON FOR THIS CAREER

As previously stated, I have gained so much inspiration from the media I consumed and I want to share what my experience inspired. Another reason I chose this career of mine is for monetary reasons,or at least for making games. I also chose to keep the cheep prices for accessability reasons, so no one has to spend so much on a game they may or may not want.
